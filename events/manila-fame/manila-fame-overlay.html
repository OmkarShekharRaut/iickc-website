About Manila FAME 2025

India International Chamber of Knowledge & Commerce (IICKC), the Authorized Partner of CITEM (Philippines), invites you to join IICKC's Official Trade Delegation to Manila FAME 2025, Asia's premier lifestyle and design sourcing show.

Why Attend Manila FAME?

  • Asia's leading lifestyle & creative expo
  • Meet top artisans & manufacturers from the Philippines
  • Explore collaborations in Furniture, Home Décor, Lifestyle, Wellness & Fashion
  • Network with international buyers and suppliers
  • Discover latest trends in design and craftsmanship

Who Should Join?

Indian importers, retailers, and business leaders in:

  • Furniture & Homeware
  • Lifestyle & Handicrafts
  • Gifts & Eco-Friendly Products
  • Wellness & Fashion Accessories
  • Interior Design & Architecture
